
| 35 | ✅ | Compile top-level expressions | `tryCompileExpression` compiles a top-level expression, or the value of a definition that is not a procedure, as a thunk the interpreter calls once -- from the interpreter, so captures and moves of frames in it finish where they should -- where it makes a procedure or loops; a form that defines at top level through `begin` stays interpreted, since wrapping would make its definitions internal. `compileProgram` and the benchmark harness use it. It was found to matter in 42: `nboyer` and `sboyer` assign every procedure from one top-level `let`, so they had never been compiled; 16.1 to 0.23 s and 19.7 to 0.22 s, ahead of Gambit compiled to JavaScript, and `quicksort`'s random numbers the same way (R84). The fuzzer's drivers are half named `let`s now, compiled as expressions; its next run found a wrong answer -- a compiled frame the interpreter resumed ran without recording the interpreter's stack, so a continuation invoked from it rewound into a `dynamic-wind` it was already in -- fixed in `CompiledFrame.step`. | R48, R84 |
